A daily bot that checks whether GTA VI is coming to PC

The PC Waiting Room is an automated YouTube channel. Every day, a bot checks official sources (Rockstar Games' website and Newswire, Take-Two Interactive's press releases, and public store listings) and the latest news to see whether Grand Theft Auto VI has been announced for PC. It then publishes a short video with what it found and lists every source in the video description.

The channel's own software uses the YouTube Data API only to upload the channel's videos, set their privacy status, and read the channel's own video statistics. It does not access, collect, or store data from viewers or from any other YouTube account.

When an official source confirms a PC version, the bot publishes one last video and stops.
